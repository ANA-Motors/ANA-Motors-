Ordering help

A few quick answers before you place an order.

How do I order?

Add the items you need, enter your contact and delivery details, then send the order through WhatsApp. We will confirm stock and the final amount.

Are delivery charges included?

No. Delivery charges and delivery time depend on your location and courier. We will confirm them before dispatch.

Can I pay by card?

Card payment is not enabled as an automatic checkout yet. Only use a verified payment link shared by ANA MOTORS after confirming the order.

How do I check if a part fits?

Send your Motors wheel size, brake type, model details or a clear photo of the existing part before ordering.
